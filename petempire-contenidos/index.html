5 signs your pet needs a dental cleaning

Thursday, October 8Chapter I · What the photo doesn't show7 slides · 4:5

Role: Educational and saveable. Teaches a home mouth check and opens the door to the dental service.

Speaks to: Owners who think bad breath is normal.

Caption
Bad breath isn't "just dog breath." 🦷

5 things to check at home — lift the lip and look:
1. Breath that stays bad after eating and drinking
2. Yellow or brown on the teeth, especially the back ones
3. A red line along the gums
4. Chewing on one side or dropping food
5. Pawing at the mouth

Bleeding, swelling or loose teeth? See your vet first. Tartar and bad breath? That's where we come in — anesthesia-free, awake, inside our van in your driveway.
