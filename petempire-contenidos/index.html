PAID · META ADS Separate from the organic grid

Meta Ads v2: mobile grooming, said plainly.

These are paid ads for Facebook and Instagram, not posts for the feed. Rebuilt after a benchmark of the mobile-grooming ads running today in the US: a carousel, a single image and a 15-second reel to find new clients, plus the dental ad for retargeting. Variants with a red dashed outline carry a “$15 off” offer and need Pet Empire’s approval.

What the benchmark shows

Meta Ad Library, United States, active ads matching “mobile grooming” (~870 results), 6 Oct 2026. About 60 ads from consumer-facing mobile groomers reviewed (equipment sellers and hiring ads excluded).

They say what it is, literally.
“Mobile Dog Grooming — We Come To You” (PurrFurred), “Skip The Drive. We Come To You.” (The Valley Grooming), “Groomed at Your Door” (Groombar). No metaphors.
They name the area in line one.
“Buda, Kyle & San Marcos dog parents…”, “San Diego pet parents…”, “Serving Marysville & nearby”. Locals stop for their own neighborhood.
They sell the pain they remove.
No car rides, no cages, no crowded salon, no waiting all day, anxious pets. “No more stressful grooming trips.”
First-visit offer is almost standard.
$10–$20 off or 10–15% off the first groom (Zoomin Groomin, Groombar, Petstar, FurFresh, Fur Exclusive, Blooming).
Low-friction CTA.
Book Now to an online booking page, Send Message / WhatsApp, or “text a photo for availability” (Becca’s Pet Mecca — the same ad running since Dec 2022).
Video and carousels dominate.
Most long-running ads are short videos (van tours, transformations, the groomer). Carousels repeat “We Come To You!” across cards.
Proof up front.
Star ratings (“⭐ 4.9-star rated”), before/after, “not Photoshop — one session”.

In the customer’s shoes

Old ad B (the van)
Beautiful, but “The salon that parks in your driveway” makes me think for a second — and on Instagram I don’t give it that second. “Grooming” is small, I never see a dog being groomed, and there is no reason to book now.
Old ad A (dental)
It stops the scroll (gross in a good way), but cold I don’t know who you are or that it’s mobile. It’s a niche service: great for people who already know you, weak as a first impression.
What I need to book
In 3 seconds: what it is (mobile pet grooming), that it comes to MY neighborhood, that it’s safe for my pet (one at a time, no cages, reviews), roughly what it costs, and one tap to book.

How to run them

  1. Campaign: Sales or Leads with Click-to-WhatsApp (14753009861) or the website booking (#book). Start with one campaign, ad sets per area.
  2. Location: Brooklyn ZIPs 11215, 11217, 11209, 11211, 11249, 11221, 11237 (Park Slope, Bay Ridge, Williamsburg, Bushwick) + 10–15 mi around New Haven, CT. People living in, not traveling to.
  3. Audience: Advantage+ audience with suggestions (dog owners, cat owners, pet grooming, dog lovers), age 25–65. Let the creative do the targeting.
  4. Creative mix: C (carousel) + D (single) + E (reel) in the prospecting ad set; A (dental) in a retargeting ad set for reel viewers (50%+), IG/FB engagers and site visitors (30 days).
  5. Budget: ~80% prospecting / 20% retargeting. Minimum ~$15–$20/day per ad set for 7 days before judging; then keep the lowest cost per WhatsApp conversation or booking.
  6. Offer: test the $15-off-first-groom variants (C6-offer, D-offer) against the no-offer versions — only if Pet Empire approves the discount.
  7. Answer fast: the ad is only as good as the reply. WhatsApp messages answered in minutes, not hours.
  8. Spanish: same ads with Spanish copy for a Spanish-language ad set (the business already offers service in Spanish).
